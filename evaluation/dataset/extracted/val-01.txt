ORIOLE REPO | COLLATERAL SCHEDULE OR-6 | 2026-10-01
All entities and trades in this schedule are fictional. Currency amounts are in thousands
of CNY unless stated otherwise. Exposure means cash principal plus accrued repo interest.
Collateral value is the clean market value after the applicable haircut; it is not the
security's face value.
HAIRCUT SCHEDULE
Sovereign bonds with remaining maturity up to 1 year: 2%.
Sovereign bonds with remaining maturity above 1 year: 4%.
Approved corporate bonds: 9%.
A corporate issue on the watch list receives an additional 6 percentage points. A missing
quote makes that issue ineligible, rather than assigning a zero haircut. The watch-list
add-on never applies to sovereign bonds.
VALUATION AND ROUNDING
Each issue is valued separately: clean market value multiplied by one minus its total
haircut. Sum the issue values without intermediate rounding, then round the total down to
the nearest 1 thousand. Accrued bond coupon is excluded. Exposure, in contrast, includes
accrued repo interest. The two kinds of accrued amounts must not be interchanged.
MARKET RECORDS
The valuation analyst stores the quote time and source with the issue code. A dealer's
indicative email may explain a price change, but is not a substitute for the approved
closing quote. A stale quote is treated under the missing-quote rule if it is outside the
permitted feed window. Custody receipts describe location and ownership; they do not
establish a market price. Reconciliation staff keep the exposure file separate from the
collateral file so that a coupon receipt cannot accidentally reduce repo principal.
MARGIN CALL
Deficit is exposure minus rounded collateral value. A call is issued only when the deficit
is strictly greater than 20. A deficit equal to 20 is logged but not called. The transfer
due on a call is the entire deficit, not just the portion above 20. The minimum transfer
threshold is a trigger, not a deductible.
VALUATION EXERCISE
Exposure is 1,000. Collateral consists of a long sovereign bond worth 500 and a
watch-listed approved corporate bond worth 500. Both have valid quotes. There are no other
securities, cash credits or pending transfers. A transfer accepted after the call is
reconciled as a new movement; it is not assumed in this snapshot.
CALL TIMING
A call delivered by 11:00 inclusive is due at 15:00 on the same business day. A later call
is due at 10:00 on the next business day. Delivery means receipt in the agreed margin
inbox, not when an analyst began drafting. A call disputed before its deadline remains due
for its undisputed amount; the disputed part is escalated to both valuation leads.
SUBSTITUTION CONTROL
A substitution requires prior counterparty consent and receipt of eligible replacement
collateral with adjusted value at least equal to the value released. The old security is
released only after custody confirms receipt. An email promising delivery is insufficient.
During a feed outage no substitution using an unquoted replacement is allowed, even if its
face value exceeds the old security's value.
VERSION CONTROL
OR-5 used a flat 10% haircut on approved corporate bonds without a watch-list add-on. OR-6
replaces that rule for every valuation dated on or after 2026-10-01, including trades
opened earlier. Sovereign haircuts are unchanged. An old trade date does not preserve the
OR-5 corporate haircut.
INFORMATION LIMIT
This schedule does not disclose actual security identifiers, the repo counterparty's legal
entity code, or a forecast of tomorrow's bond price. The exercise is a closed fictional
snapshot and does not authorize real transfers.
