MORROW ACQUIRING | OPERATING NOTE M-4 | 2026-08-01
Fictional merchant settlement rules. Currency: CNY. A settlement batch and a dispute case
have different identifiers. A case reference is not a payout instruction. This note governs
reserve movements, not cardholder credit limits.
BATCH CLOCK
Transactions accepted before 18:00 enter that business day's batch; exactly 18:00 enters
the next business day's batch. Settlement is on T+2 business days. Saturdays, Sundays and
the published closed days are skipped. The examples below assume no closed days other than
weekends.
RESERVE MATRIX
Standard merchant: withhold 6% of gross accepted sales.
Elevated merchant: withhold 12% of gross accepted sales.
A merchant with both flags uses Elevated only, not 18%. Refunds do not reduce the reserve
base of the batch in which they are paid. Transaction fees are separate deductions and do
not reduce the reserve base either.
LEDGER DISCIPLINE
Operations reconcile accepted sale IDs to the terminal report before sending the batch for
review. A void made before acceptance never becomes a gross sale. A refund made after
acceptance is a separate debit, even when its amount equals the original sale. Reviewers
may attach card scheme messages as context; those messages cannot replace a matched sale
ID. A manual note does not change an accepted timestamp. Archived batch reports retain the
original risk flag to explain historical reserves.
WORKED BATCH
A Standard merchant has gross accepted sales of 50,000, refunds of 8,000, transaction fees
of 500 and a matured old reserve release of 1,000. No other debit applies. Payout equals
gross sales minus refunds, fees and the new reserve, plus the matured release. An unrelated
open dispute has no deduction until a provisional debit is posted.
DISPUTE PATH
A provisional dispute debit is posted when a case is opened with a scheme debit notice. The
merchant has 7 calendar days after notice to submit a matched receipt and delivery proof. A
response with only a receipt is incomplete. Winning a case reverses the provisional debit;
it does not create another sale or reduce a reserve that was correctly withheld.
RESERVE RELEASE
Each reserve lot matures 90 calendar days after its batch date. Release requires maturity
and no open dispute linked to that lot. An unlinked dispute does not block that lot. Lots
are tracked separately: one blocked lot cannot justify retaining every other mature lot. No
interest is paid on held reserves under M-4.
OUTAGE EXCEPTION
A documented terminal clock outage may move an accepted transaction back by one batch only
after both reconciliation and risk officers approve. It cannot move a transaction across
two batches. The outage exception does not shorten T+2 settlement or the 90-day reserve
maturity period.
VERSION REGISTER
M-3 used 5% for Standard merchants. M-4 uses 6% for batches dated on or after 2026-08-01.
Existing M-3 reserve lots keep their original amounts; they are not topped up. The 90-day
maturity rule is unchanged. Do not recalculate an old lot using the current withholding
rate.
SCOPE LIMIT
This fictional note does not list actual merchant bank account numbers, the card scheme's
arbitration fee, or the issuer's internal fraud score. Those items cannot be derived from
reserve percentages or settlement dates.
